## Acceptance and stopping

The known local date-fidelity regression must be repaired in the complete
five-repetition comparison. Do not accept another5/5-to2/5 result merely because
aggregate performance rises. Check explicit conversion and supplied absolute-date
transfer cases for overrestriction. A substantial new workflow/scope regression
also requires repair. Report isolated one-trial differences and uncertainty rather
than claiming causal certainty from five samples.

For this version, early rejection is explicitly allowed after a complete local
five-repetition date family still shows the known substantive regression, or a
new confirmed scope/permission blocker is found. Preserve the entire incomplete
version and restart a new version, never selected favorable trials. Otherwise,
finish both models and all families before a final acceptance decision or PR.
