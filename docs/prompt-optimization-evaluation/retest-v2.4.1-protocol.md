## Acceptance and early rejection

The accepted candidate must pass all five local factual-progress trials,
including date consistency and the new final-sentence contract. A retained
wrong date or missing required weekday does not become success because an
aggregate rises. Explicit calendar conversion must remain usable, and supplied
absolute dates must remain intact. Require no new unrequested API-signature
break in an otherwise completed, positionally correct repair.

Early rejection is allowed on the first definitive recurrence of the known
wrong-date/lost-weekday failure in that final local family, a confirmed new API
signature break as described above, or a new scope/permission blocker. Preserve
the whole partial version and every available result. A replacement version
requires a new source freeze and whole comparison, not selected favorable
reruns. This is an engineering acceptance rule, not a statistical test.

Otherwise complete both models and every family before accepting the candidate
or creating its PR. The local aggregate must recover from the previously
reported regression. Report isolated differences, formatting failures and
uncertainty; do not infer universal reliability from five repetitions. Review
substantial workflow regressions and resolve in-scope implementation gaps.
